Two points block distribution. The others degrade the kit without preventing it from going out.

## Blocking

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1 · Referral codes do not exist" icon="circle-x">
    **Impact: the kit cannot be distributed.**

    Every call to action, across the seven posts and the two emails, points at `cyruss.ai/[YOUR-CODE]`. An agent publishing today sends their network to a URL that does not exist.

    **To settle:** who generates the codes, in what format, and by what date? Without an answer, everything else is theoretical.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="2 · Three unverified commercial promises" icon="circle-x">
    **Impact: risk transferred onto the agents.**

    Kit v1 promised a founder rate "locked for life" and a "limited cohort". Agents repeat these phrases to their own peers, under their own name. If the rate is not genuinely perpetual, or if the cohort cap is invented scarcity, it is the agent who loses credibility, not CYRUSS.

    **Interim treatment:** both mentions have been **removed** from the posts and the emails in this version. They can return as soon as CYRUSS confirms in writing: is the rate locked for life, and is the cohort cap real, at what number?
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## To correct

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="3 · The visual briefs contradicted the charter" icon="wrench">
    **Corrected in this version.**

    The charter block had been updated against Brand Guidelines v1.0 (Figtree, `#75FBAA`, `#FFC7FE`), but the six briefs below it still carried the old specification: `#0F2E23`, `#0B7A57`, "emerald" and **Poppins**. A designer follows the brief, not the header: all six visuals would have come out off-charter.

    The briefs have been rewritten against the official charter. **To verify:** that no visual has already been produced against the old specification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4 · The kit broke its own rule" icon="wrench">
    **Corrected in this version.**

    Part 4 forbids agents from writing any figure they cannot source. Part 1 nonetheless had them publishing "documentary fraud has exploded" and "limited cohort". The "+500%" statistic had been removed for exactly this reason, but the softer formulations had survived.

    They are removed, and the guide's rule now names them explicitly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="4b · What is removed is &quot;locked for life&quot;, not &quot;founder rate&quot;" icon="circle-check">
    **No substantive correction needed. Recorded here to prevent a third round trip.**

    The distinction is easy to lose, and it has already caused one false correction: golden rule #2 on the Introduction was once "corrected" to strip the founder rate, then restored.

    Point 2 does not remove the founder rate. It removes the **two promises that surrounded it**: the rate **"locked for life"** (a perpetuity commitment) and the **"limited cohort"** or "limited places" (unverified scarcity).

    The **founder rate remains an existing, mentionable offer**: in golden rule #2 on the Introduction, and in the safe-formulations list in the Usage guide. That is also the state of the local document `CYRUSS-Kit-Partage-Ambassadeurs.md`, aligned to the web version on 13 August.

    **What changed in this version:** the Guide and the Emails page now name the two prohibited promises explicitly, instead of saying "mentions of founder rate and limited cohort" — an ambiguous phrasing that implied the rate itself was off-limits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5 · The logo SVG is missing" icon="wrench">
    The visuals use a placeholder logo block. Visual 6, the badge, is the only one where the logo fills the frame: that is the one that genuinely requires the vector file.

    **Expected from Marvin:** official SVG, plus the white and knockout variants.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="5b · Icons were not rendering" icon="wrench">
    **Corrected in this version.**

    `docs.json` declared no icon library, so Mintlify fell back to its Font Awesome default while every icon in the kit was written with Lucide names. Only the names common to both libraries resolved; the rest rendered blank in the sidebar.

    `icons.library` is now set to `lucide`, and two stale names were replaced (`hashtag` → `hash`, `help-circle` → `circle-help`).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## To decide

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="6 · Nothing tells us which post works" icon="chart-no-axes-column">
    Seven posts, two emails, and a single link per agent. There is no way to know whether the fraud angle beats the selection angle, or which channel converts.

    **Proposal:** one UTM parameter per variant, for example `?utm_content=li-landlord-b`, appended to the personal link. The ambassador programme then becomes a source of evidence, on the same footing as the organic test in the Google Ads plan. Without it, we distribute and hope.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="7 · Delivering the visuals" icon="chart-no-axes-column">
    Six visuals, six separate Canva files, each to be personalised with a photo, a name and a link. That is a lot of friction for a non-technical agent.

    **Proposal, already stated on the Visuals page:** deliver each visual as a **shareable Canva template**. The agent duplicates and replaces, with nothing to download. To be confirmed on the CYRUSS side, since the files live in their workspace.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="8 · The kit is currently in English" icon="languages">
    This version of the site has been translated to English for approval. The agent-facing kit ships in **French**.

    The French original is preserved on the `main` branch and in merged PR #1. It must be restored before the link is shared: the copy blocks are what agents paste, so in English they are illustrative only.

    Note for the restore: the Usage guide's formal / informal section is the *tu / vous* rule in French, and it is the one section where the English cannot carry the full meaning. Check it specifically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
