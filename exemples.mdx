## Sending your screenshots

<Steps>
  <Step title="Publish, then capture" icon="camera">
    Capture the post as it appears in the feed, with your name visible. No draft screenshots: we want the real render, not the editor preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the file" icon="tag">
    Format `channel-angle-name.png`, for example `linkedin-landlord-b-dupont.png`. It saves guesswork at the other end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it to Boost33" icon="upload">
    It replaces the matching frame and becomes the reference example for the other agents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Danger>
  **Blur third parties.** If a comment, a private message or a landlord's name appears in your screenshot, mask it before sending. We are publishing your post, not your clients' data.
</Danger>
