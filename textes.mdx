<Warning>
  **English review version.** The copy blocks below are translations. Agents publish the French originals — an English LinkedIn post is not usable in this market. Restore the French from `main` before distribution.
</Warning>

Every block has a copy button. Replace `[your link]` with your personal link before publishing.

<Note>
  Several variants per channel, deliberately. If every agent publishes the same post in the same week, the effect cancels itself out. Pick one and add a sentence of your own.
</Note>

## Which post for whom

| You want to reach | Channel | Variant | Register |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landlords | LinkedIn | Selection angle **or** fraud angle | Formal |
| Fellow agents | LinkedIn, WhatsApp | Business Club | Formal |
| Tenants | Instagram, Facebook | Tenant caption | Informal |
| Your direct network | WhatsApp, SMS | Direct share | Either |

<Tip>
  You do not have to use all of them. An agent who sustains **one** variant a week beats an agent who publishes all seven at once and then disappears.
</Tip>

    ```text Fraud angle
    Fake payslips, fake tax notices, generated by AI in two minutes. 😳

    Spotting them has become part of my job, and honestly, with the naked eye it is almost impossible.
    ```

      **The more aggressive variant.** It works well, but do not enrich it with figures. No "+500%", no "fraud has exploded". You are describing what you see in your own work, not quoting a statistic.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Fellow agents">

  On WhatsApp, do not broadcast these to your whole contact list. A message that is obviously bulk gets noticed, and costs you more credibility than it earns you contacts. Send them one at a time, to the people they are genuinely useful for.
</Warning>

## Making it yours, in three moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="Replace the link" icon="link">
    `[your link]` becomes your personal link. It is the only mandatory substitution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a lived detail" icon="message-square-quote">
    Something like "last week a landlord told me…". That is what separates a recommendation from an advert.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it aloud" icon="volume-2">
    If a sentence does not sound like you, rewrite it. Your network knows how you talk.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="What if I want to write my own from scratch?" icon="pencil">
  Go ahead. Just keep the kit's four markers: you stay at the centre, CYRUSS is the tool, one call to action, and no figure you cannot source.

  The safe formulations: verified files, business network, founder rate, time saved, confidence gained. See [Usage guide](/guide) for the full list of what never to write.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  Copy pasted at 100% is obvious. Always add a lived detail, something like "last week a landlord told me…". That is what separates a recommendation from an advert.
</Tip>
