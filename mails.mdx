## To a landlord in your network

**Subject:** A safer way to let your property

<Accordion title="Other possible subject lines" icon="inbox">
  - Your candidates, verified before they reach you
  - Letting without sorting 200 applications
</Accordion>

```text Landlord email
Hello [first name],

I am writing because your property could benefit from the way I now work.

I select tenants through a certified rental passport: every candidate is verified (identity, income, solvency) before they are even presented to you. In practice, you only receive reliable applications, and you choose with confidence, without the risk of a forged file.

If you have a property to let, I would like to show you how it works. A 15-minute call this week?

Kind regards,
[signature]
```

<Check>
  This email asks for **15 minutes**, not a signature. That is what makes it easy to accept. Do not replace that ask with "would you like to know more?", which commits to nothing and gets nothing.
</Check>

## To a fellow agent

**Subject:** Less sorting, more mandates

<Accordion title="Other possible subject lines" icon="inbox">
  - Files arrive verified
  - A network of mandates between agents
</Accordion>

```text Fellow-agent email
Hello [first name],

I am part of the CYRUSS Business Club and I think it could be of interest to you.

In practice: a network that circulates mandates, tenant files that are already verified, and commission on what you bring in. It does not replace your job — it takes away the thankless part, sorting applications, and gives you back your advisory time.

10 minutes for me to show you?

[signature]
```

<Note>
  The French original uses the informal register here, because it is written for a fellow agent you know well. If that is not the case, switch it back to formal — that is the default for fellow agents. See [Usage guide](/guide).
</Note>

## Before you hit send

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check both substitutions" icon="search">
    `[first name]` and `[signature]`. An email arriving with `[first name]` still in it undoes all the credibility this kit is trying to build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a line of context" icon="message-square-quote">
    "We met at the March viewing", "you entrusted me with the two-bed on Rue Gambetta". Two seconds to write, and the email stops being a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send them one at a time" icon="send">
    No blind-copying thirty contacts. An email that is visibly bulk gets spotted and deleted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up once, not three times" icon="clock">
    One short follow-up after five days, on the same thread. No reply after that and you let it go and move on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="Follow-up template" icon="reply">
  ```text Follow-up at 5 days
  Hello [first name],

  Just bringing my message back up in case it arrived at a bad moment.

  If the topic is not relevant right now, tell me and I will not press.

  Kind regards,
  [signature]
  ```

  "Tell me and I will not press" earns a lot of replies, including clear noes. A clear no is worth more than a dead thread.
</Accordion>

<Warning>
  Mentions of a rate **"locked for life"** and a **"limited cohort"** have been removed from these templates pending written confirmation from CYRUSS. The **founder rate** itself remains mentionable — it is an existing offer.

  Do not add the two promises back on your own initiative. You are the one repeating them under your own name, so you are the one who loses credibility if they do not hold. See [Open items](/a-resoudre).
</Warning>
