## Typeface

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Headings" icon="heading">
    **Figtree Bold 700**

    Post headlines, hooks, cover slides.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Subheadings" icon="type">
    **Figtree Medium 500**

    Subheadings, captions, credits.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Body" icon="align-left">
    **Figtree Regular 400**

    Body copy, lists, paragraphs.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Check>
  Figtree is free in Canva and on Google Fonts. There is nothing to buy and nothing to install.
</Check>

## Logo

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Construction" icon="shapes">
    Interlocking circles, then "CYRUSS" in Figtree Bold. Always present on a visual, but discreet: in a corner or along the bottom.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Clear space" icon="square-dashed">
    Equal to **0.5× the height of the logo** on all four sides. Nothing enters it: no text, no photo, no frame edge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Minimum size" icon="ruler">
    **120 px wide** in digital. Below that the interlocking circles become illegible and the logo reads as a smudge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Variants" icon="layers">
    Standard version on light backgrounds. **White** on dark backgrounds and over photography. **Knockout** on coloured backgrounds. Always on its own layer, never flattened into the background.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
  **The official SVG is still missing.** Templates use a placeholder logo block pending the files from Marvin: standard SVG, plus white and knockout variants.
</Warning>

## The prohibitions

<Danger>
  Four mistakes that take a visual outside the charter.
</Danger>

| Prohibited | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **No emoji in the visuals** | In post copy, yes. On the image, no. |
| **No gold or brass** | It is not a CYRUSS colour. Also avoid interior photography dominated by gold tones. |
| **No unsourced figure** | Not on the image, not in the caption. See [Usage guide](/guide). |
| **No typeface other than Figtree** | Poppins appears in older briefs. That was an error, now corrected. |

## The style in one line

<Tip>
  **Clean, airy, modern.** If you are torn between adding an element and leaving white space, leave the white space.
</Tip>
