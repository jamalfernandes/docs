## Formal or informal?

<Note>
  French distinguishes *tu* from *vous*; English does not. In the French original this section is the *tu / vous* rule, and it is one of the sharpest rules in the kit. Read "formal" as **vous** and "informal" as **tu** — and check this section carefully when the French is restored, because it is the one place where the translation cannot carry the full meaning.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Formal · vous">
    **For landlords and fellow agents.**

    It is the brand's voice, and what a landlord entrusting you with a property expects. A fellow agent too, even once they become a friend.

    <Check>
      This is the default column. When in doubt, use it.
    </Check>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Informal · tu">
    **For tenants on Instagram and WhatsApp.**

    Younger audience, informal channel. The formal register sounds bureaucratic there.

    <Warning>
      This is the exception, not the rule. Two channels, one audience. Everywhere else, formal.
    </Warning>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Danger>
  One rule to remember: **within a single post, you never switch.** A text that starts formal and ends informal reads like a badly proofread copy-paste, which is generally what it is.
</Danger>

## When to publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="One post a week is enough" icon="calendar">
    Consistency beats volume. Better one post held every week than five at once and then nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The carousel on Tuesday or Thursday" icon="trending-up">
    On LinkedIn those are the two days that carry this format best.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The story whenever, the WhatsApp card on demand" icon="zap">
    As soon as a contact is looking to rent, the WhatsApp card is the fastest answer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Accordion title="A starting rhythm over four weeks" icon="calendar-days">
  Nothing compulsory, but it saves you the blank page on Monday.

  | Week | What you publish | Channel |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 | Install your banner, then the "selection angle" square post | LinkedIn |
  | 2 | The five-slide carousel | LinkedIn, Instagram |
  | 3 | The "Club member" story, plus the tenant post | Instagram |
  | 4 | The "fraud angle" post, then a message to fellow agents | LinkedIn, WhatsApp |

  Then you start again from the top with a different variant.
</Accordion>

## Good habits

- **Always personalise.** Add a sentence of your own, something you have lived. Copy pasted at 100% is obvious.
- **Reply in the comments and in private.** The share is only the front door; the conversation does the rest.
- **One call to action** per post: your link.

## What you must never write

<Warning>
  You publish under your own name. These phrases expose you, not CYRUSS.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Performance promises" icon="circle-x">
    "Guaranteed instant certification", "3× more chances". Unproven.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Any figure you cannot source" icon="circle-x">
    If you do not know where it comes from, it does not go out. That includes "fraud has exploded" and "places are limited" until someone has confirmed it to you in writing.

    Soft formulations count too: "many", "most", "more and more". Those are figures in disguise.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The finished product" icon="circle-x">
    "The app does everything automatically." It rolls out in stages. See [Questions you'll be asked](/faq).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A letting outcome" icon="circle-x">
    Anything like "you'll let it in 48 hours". You control neither the market, nor the property, nor the price.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="&quot;Locked for life&quot; and &quot;limited cohort&quot;" icon="circle-x">
    The **founder rate exists and you can talk about it.** What is removed from the kit are the two promises around it: the rate **"locked for life"** (a perpetuity commitment) and the **"limited cohort"** or "limited places" (unverified scarcity).

    They come back if CYRUSS confirms in writing that the rate really is locked for life, and that the cohort cap is real, at a specific number. Until then, do not reintroduce them on your own initiative.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Check>
  **What you can say without risk:** verified files, business network, founder rate, time saved, confidence gained.
</Check>

<Tip>
  The one-question test: *if a landlord asks me where I got this, do I have an answer?* If not, it does not go out.
</Tip>

## And then what?

Someone replies. Now what?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply privately the same day" icon="clock">
    Interest fades fast. A reply tomorrow is worth half a reply within the hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Qualify with one question" icon="circle-help">
    "Do you have a property to let right now, or are you preparing for later?" The answer tells you whether this needs a call or just a follow-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Offer a specific slot" icon="calendar-check">
    Not "call me whenever", but "Thursday at 2pm, does that work?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Sign-ups go through your personal link, so your referrals and your Club commissions are counted automatically. There is nothing for you to declare manually.
</Note>
