<Info>
  These four questions come up almost every time, often as a DM within the hour of your post. Having the answer ready changes everything.
</Info>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What exactly is CYRUSS?" icon="circle-help">
    A certified rental passport. The tenant builds their verified file once, then shares it anywhere. On the landlord and agent side, you only receive reliable candidates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does it cost the tenant?" icon="wallet">
    Creating the file is accessible to everyone. Professional services are on subscription.

    <Warning>
      Do not quote a figure. The pricing grid is not confirmed for distribution, and a price announced wrongly is very hard to walk back.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is this better than DossierFacile?" icon="git-compare">
    DossierFacile **assembles** the file. CYRUSS **certifies** it and **connects** it to a network. Reliability is verified, not just tidied.

    You can mention DossierFacile in conversation. Avoid naming it in a public post: a direct comparison usually rebounds on whoever makes it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it all work already?" icon="construction">
    Answer honestly: features roll out in waves. What exists today is the network, the verified files and the support.

    That is the right answer because it still holds in six months. Promising a finished product exposes you at the first missing feature.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The harder questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="&quot;Is it even legal to check my income like that?&quot;" icon="scale">
    Yes. The tenant builds their **own** file and chooses **for themselves** who to share it with. Nothing is verified behind their back, nothing is passed on without their action.

    If the question goes deeper than that, do not improvise as a lawyer: refer them to CYRUSS.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="&quot;Are you paid to talk about this?&quot;" icon="handshake">
    Answer yes, plainly. You are a Business Club member and there is commission on the sign-ups you bring in.

    Denying something checkable costs infinitely more than owning it. "Yes, and it is also the tool I work with every day" is an answer that holds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="&quot;Another thing to fill in…&quot;" icon="frown">
    That is an objection of fatigue, not of substance. The answer is the "once only": the file is built once and then shared everywhere, instead of being redone for every viewing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A question you do not have the answer to" icon="message-square-warning">
    "I don't want to give you a wrong answer — let me check and come back to you." Then you ask Boost33 or CYRUSS.

    <Check>
      Always better than an invented answer. An "I'll check" builds your credibility; an approximation destroys it.
    </Check>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
